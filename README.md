# AL-BHURANI — Sweets & Dry Items Franchise POS & Barcode System

> **Luxury Black & Royal Gold Theme (TENS11 Design System)**  
> Built for **AL-BHURANI Sweets, Namkeen & Indian Dry Items** franchise outlets with complete Barcode Integration, POS Quick-Billing, Stock Tracking, and Daily Accounts.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Highlights

### 1. ⚡ Live Barcode Integration (Plug-and-Play)
- **Zero Drivers / Zero Setup Needed**: Any standard USB or Wireless Barcode Scanner Gun (₹900–₹1,500 on Amazon) works as an HID keyboard out-of-the-box.
- **Webcam / Phone Camera Scanner**: Scan barcodes directly using your laptop webcam or smartphone camera via browser.
- **Interactive Quick-Zap Demo Bar**: Click on sample barcodes (*Kaju Katli, Motichoor Ladoo, Ratlam Sev, California Badam*) to simulate instant scanning with realistic retail POS chime/beep audio!
- **Universal Hardware Diagnostic Bench**: A dedicated test screen where you can plug any physical scanner and see the exact decoded digits and transfer speed in milliseconds.

### 2. 🏷️ In-House Barcode Sticker & Thermal Label Generator
- Generate crisp **50mm × 35mm** or **50mm × 25mm** thermal labels for fresh in-store sweets packs and loose namkeen pouches.
- Contains:
  - **AL-BHURANI SWEETS & DRY ITEMS** branding
  - Product Name & Net Weight (250g, 500g, 1kg)
  - Batch Number, Packaging Date, and Expiry Date
  - High-precision scannable **CODE128** Barcode
  - MRP (₹) with tax breakdown
- Supports standard thermal label printers (*TVS LP 46 Neo, TSC TE244, Citizen, Xprinter*).

### 3. 🛒 High-Speed POS Billing & Thermal Receipts
- Auto-focus scanner input with sound synthesis.
- Fast cart adjustments (Qty `+` / `-`, Discounts `5%` / `10%`).
- 5% GST calculation for sweets & namkeen.
- Payment modes: **Cash** (with instant change calculator), **UPI QR**, and **Card Swipes**.
- Instant 80mm thermal receipt print preview with store header, bill number, cashier info, and items.

### 4. 📦 Stock & Inventory Control
- Track real-time stock units.
- **Low Stock Alerts**: Visual warning badges when stock falls below threshold.
- Quick inline stock editor and "Add New Sweet/Snack" modal with auto-generated EAN-13 barcodes.

### 5. 💰 Finance & Daily Accounts
- Today's Total Revenue, Cash in Drawer, UPI Collections, and Card Totals.
- Store Outflows & Expense Tracker (Ghee/Mawa, Boxes, Electricity, Staff Wages).
- Recent Bills table with 1-click receipt re-printing.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

1. Open PowerShell or Command Prompt in the project folder:
   ```powershell
   cd C:\$$$\AL-BHURANI
   ```

2. Start the local server:
   ```powershell
   npm run dev
   ```

3. Open your browser:
   👉 **[http://localhost:5175/](http://localhost:5175/)**

## 🎯 How to Give an Impressive Demo to the Client

1. **Start on the POS Screen**:
   - Point out the royal **TENS11 Luxury Gold Aesthetic** which elevates the brand look.
   - Click any item in the **"Demo Quick-Zap Bar"** at the top. The software will play a crisp **"BEEP"**, instantly add the item to the bill, and calculate the total.
2. **Show Hardware Flexibility**:
   - If you have any USB barcode scanner gun, plug it in and zap any physical packet — it will add the product automatically!
   - Click **"Camera Scan"** to show them how staff can even use a phone or laptop camera.
3. **Show Barcode Printing**:
   - Switch to **"Stock & Items"** tab.
   - Click **"Print Sticker"** on *Kaju Katli Special (500g)*.
   - Show them the ready-to-print 50mm thermal sticker with their brand name, expiry date, MRP, and scannable barcode!
4. **Show Finance & Accounts**:
   - Switch to **"Finance & Sales"** to show cash in drawer vs UPI collections and recent bills.
5. **Show the Barcode Guide**:
   - Switch to **"Barcode Guide"** to show them the hardware recommendations (TVS printers and scanners) so they feel confident about hardware procurement.
